# Incident Severity Policy
Tags: policy, severity

## Severity levels
critical: full outage or data loss affecting most customers. high: major feature unavailable or revenue impact. medium: degraded feature with a workaround. low: minor issue with no customer impact.

## Response targets
critical: acknowledge within 5 minutes, update every 15 minutes. high: acknowledge within 15 minutes, update every 30 minutes. medium: acknowledge within 1 hour. low: next business day.

## Choosing severity
Pick the higher severity when unsure and downgrade after investigation. Do not open an incident for a single transient alert that recovered on its own.
