# On-Call Handbook
Tags: on-call, handbook

## Starting a shift
Review open incidents, recent deployments, and any handover notes. Confirm the paging tool works.

## During an alert
Acknowledge, check the service status, follow the service runbook, and post updates in the incident channel. Escalate early rather than late.

## Ending a shift
Write a handover note listing open issues, mitigations in place, and follow-ups.
